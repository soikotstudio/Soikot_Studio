# Project Guidelines & Negative Design Constraints

The following constraints are strictly enforced for Soikot Studio:

## Forbidden Items & Anti-Patterns
- Harsh gradients & rainbow coloring
- Neon colors & basic pastel colors
- Purple and black color schemes
- Pure white background (#ffffff stark)
- Drop shadows & liquid glass (glassmorphism)
- Radial orbs & dot grids
- Colored left stripe accents & bubbly soft corner radiuses
- Hover animations & animated bouncing arrows
- Lucide icons, sparkle icons, & emojis
- Terminal window mockups
- Bento grids & three feature cards in a row
- Three pricing tiers & skeleton loaders
- Inter, Geist, and Space Grotesk fonts
- Em dashes (—)
- "It's not X, it's Y" copywriting & checkmark bullets
- Fake testimonials & fake/missing product demos
- Missing Terms of Service (TOS) or Privacy Policy
